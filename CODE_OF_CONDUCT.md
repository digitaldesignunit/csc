# Code of Conduct

Welcome to the development repository of the Catalog of Second Chances (CSC), a research prototype of the
Digital Design Unit (DDU) at TU Darmstadt. CSC is built by researchers, students and practice partners who
document reused building components together. This document says how we work with each other and how to report
a problem. How to contribute is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); how to report a vulnerability is in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background, discipline,
level of experience, age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, nationality, religion or
sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews and every other project space.
- Assume good intent, ask before you judge, and accept that people from architecture, engineering and software
  read the same thing differently.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person; accept feedback the same way.
- Credit the work of others: data, photos, code and ideas.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, e-mail, phone numbers, photos of people) without
  their explicit permission.
- Uploading content you have no right to share: photos, drawings, documents or catalogue data owned by others.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits), on the catalogue website
(https://2ndchances.build) and its API, in the Grasshopper interface, and wherever someone represents the project.
On the catalogue it also covers what you contribute: records, photos, attachments and comments, and how you use the
datasets you moderate (invite only people who expect it).

## Reporting

- **Conduct:** write to the contact address in the imprint (https://2ndchances.build/imprint). Reports are read by
  the maintainers only and are handled confidentially.
- **Personal data in the catalogue:** to have personal data in a record redacted or an account deleted, write to
  the same address (see the privacy page, https://2ndchances.build/privacy).
- **Security:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never open a public issue for a vulnerability.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what counts as a breach of this code and act in proportion: a note, a warning, the removal
of a comment, record, photo or attachment, the withdrawal of a dataset role, or a temporary or permanent ban from
the repository and the catalogue. Moderators of a dataset act for their dataset in the same spirit and can ask the
maintainers to step in.
